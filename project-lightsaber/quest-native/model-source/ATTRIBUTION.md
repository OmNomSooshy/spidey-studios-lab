# Luke Skywalker ROTJ hilt model

Title: Luke Skywalker ROTJ Lightsaber 3D Model  
Author: Worthy (@worthy_edge)  
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/luke-skywalker-rotj-lightsaber-914b5939b240401b9d42938dfb86f9f5  
License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The supplied GLB is retained unchanged as `luke-rotj.glb`. The build converts
its triangle geometry to the app's compact GLES mesh format, rotates the model
axis onto the controller's local -Z saber axis, and moves the origin to the
center of its ribbed grip. The original geometry and material factors are kept.
