## Restraint and truth

An absence has at most one outcome and may have none. First tuning: at least 90 seconds away, a 42% possibility only after a valid physical opportunity exists, an 18-minute persisted cooldown, no consecutive repetition of an act family, and no further outcome while previous evidence remains unseen. Absences older than 30 days are declined. These are safety/surprise limits, not return incentives.

Departure must show an awake, settled, screen-gravity Byte with no grab, held/carried object, pointer intent, rope, nest, sensor doze/bask, journey, room travel, expedition, prank, care activity, running water or open modal. Return must also be free. Hidden/pagehide listeners save the proof before existing interruption handlers clear ownership. Real pointer input or a transition into busy/sleep state immediately invalidates an idle checkpoint, including the case where Chrome is subsequently killed without pagehide.

The saved home blob must still match exactly. Another live viewer vetoes resolution. No witnessed event is relabelled as unseen, and the renderer never advances invisible time. Room travel alone does not generate XI events.

Object provenance is conservative. Hand handling records Human authorship; visible Byte invitation/retrieval or a genuine loose-food shove establishes Byte authorship. An in-hand refusal only makes broccoli an offer when released near Byte, without a portal transition. Deliberately placing it elsewhere protects it. Legacy placements are unknown and protected. Stored/unowned/inaccessible toys, absent/empty food and bought treats are excluded.

Persistent state is one absence proof, one last receipt and at most three small residue records, plus the existing objects' provenance/wrapping fields. Wallet, equipment, trophies, food replenishment and ownership remain their existing systems. Moved objects and bite quantities persist independently of the bounded decorative residue. No event-history database or generic life engine.

## Rendering and physical inspection

The packet, small residue images and dangling strand are pre-rendered. Residue caching is bounded to three small images (under 0.3 MB including packet and shared strand). No full-screen layer, per-frame gradient/pattern generation or hidden simulation. Four-second checkpoints serialize only an eligible idle home state; active expeditions do not incur this new checkpoint work. The visible lease remains cheap.

Silk packet presentation shares the actual food transform and collision body. Residue is decoration with real sponge-contact cleanup; it is not an active web, prescribed anchor or newly simulated prop. The existing player/food/toy ropes remain untouched. Partial cleanup persists separately for the table and shelf.

## Revisions and rejected ideas

- The first picnic position was genuinely occupied by broccoli after ordinary play. XI refused the overlap; three safe table choices now provide an alternative without moving the existing vegetable.
- A packet beneath Byte was hard to read. The far table-end position preserves the food physics and clears body/passage territory.
- The first companion slot could sit beneath mute. Literal touch inspection, body clearance and five-size tests replaced the screenshot-only assumption.
- Repeated per-frame residue paths had a measurable constrained-render cost. Tiny bounded cached images replace them.
- One sponge contact initially cleared an entire two-location trace. Cleanup now preserves the physical distinction between table crumbs and pantry silk.
- Fixture scripts attempting storage on `about:blank` and a throw check contended by simultaneous browser work were harness failures. Origin guards and isolated trusted-touch reruns fixed those; product physics was not changed to pass the harness. Resource-contended performance samples are retained outside the repository but excluded from the final comparison.
- Rejected: a hidden/offline autonomous simulation; random possession rearrangement; automatic unseen washing; fabricated sleep/nest tableaux; a pantry-hoard microstory chain; an active cocoon rope. None earned its authority or machinery relative to these three actual aftermaths.

## Known limits / Human Eyes decisions

This first sparse vocabulary only resolves an absence in a relevant existing room. It does not populate every room or model a whole day. Conservative migration/provenance can make an established household initially quiet until real Byte activity or genuine pantry replenishment establishes an eligible opportunity. Frequency, packet readability and whether these outcomes feel like his life require Human Fingers.

Loose decorative residue is bounded to the latest three traces; the actual moved possessions, quantities and wrapper state remain independent. Sleeping on departure never authors incompatible activity, but existing X cold-reload wake semantics remain existing X semantics. Clearing browser data clears this local household history, as before. Storage denial disables persistence without preventing the creature from arriving.

No Android work, new room, economy, needs loop, notification or generalized narrative architecture. Additional traces would now be content breadth rather than proof of the illusion. Cut the engine after the validated candidate is published; Human Eyes decides what survives.
