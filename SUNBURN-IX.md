# Sunburn IX — a life that leaves little traces

Candidate branch: `experiment/sunburn-ix-2026-10-07`, based on VIII's validated trunk-placement correction `c5e417bdb9c21e54b3395556f8ee75496d0219c4`. Canonical and earlier experimental branches remain separate. The candidate reuses the existing private phone origin to preserve local belongings, wallet, wardrobe and history.

## The answer pursued

Byte's existing life now leaves small, connected evidence. A treat is a real portion on his kitchen saucer, something he can anticipate, taste, get on his face, have washed off, and later dream about. A returned toy, an expedition fragment and getting dressed earn a brief self-satisfied look. Washing gives him a moment of clean contentment; wet scuttling leaves temporary little footprints. The house gains restrained handmade details rather than new rooms or new chores.

No need, happiness, nutrition or emotion statistics were introduced. The new presentation reads existing causal events. It does not own Byte's forces, movement, touch, rope or decisions.

## What is playable

**Dreaming:** after 1.5 seconds of genuine sleep, a small thought cloud fades in above the actual sleeping body. It can show the food he actually tasted or rejected, an owned toy, his Crystal history or bath sponge. The last meaningful context persists; the dream alternates inexpensive images after eight seconds. The authored asleep pose and physical sleeping web remain authoritative. Grabbing or waking him removes the dream. Dozing is not treated as genuine sleep. Dreams have no interaction or rewards.

**Little Treasures:** the original 43 permanent items remain, with six repeatable treats added. A purchase produces one real portion on the kitchen saucer. Buying while in the kitchen closes the shop to expose the physical moment immediately. Buying elsewhere leaves the receipt and delivers to the same kitchen. The portion can be held, offered, withdrawn, thrown, carried through the house, left for Byte, partly eaten, or consumed using the existing mouth/contact and autonomous-food grammar. Actual bites visibly diminish it. Uneaten/partial portions and wallet debit survive reload, including delivery during an existing room transition. Treats never become owned/equipped cosmetics and never enter free biscuit replenishment.

## Rendering comparison

Matched software Chromium scenes at DPR2 compare the exact VIII parent with IX, without concurrent browser tests. Outdoor IX measured 58.6–59.7 FPS versus VIII 57.8–58.0 at 1×; at 6× CPU throttling IX varied 27.6–36.0 versus VIII 32.2–34.9. Crystal cache stayed eight tiles / 9,960,040 bitmap bytes. This variation is reported rather than claimed to be identical handset performance.

Self-QA exposed an expensive initial sleeping-room detail layer: IX fell to 9.6 FPS versus VIII 18.8 at 6×. Controlled ablation retained the dream but removed the detail layer and recovered 17.9 FPS; removing only the dream left 10.2. The full-screen transparent detail bitmap was the cost. Cropping the cache once preserved exact rendered pixels and reduced the latest matched sleeping scene to 48.9 ms median completed raster versus its earlier 87.1 ms. In the rerun IX measured 16.8 FPS versus VIII 14.6 under 6×, and both 60 at 1×. A deliberately saturated kitchen scene with 18 bubbles and 16 footprints measured IX 47.2 versus VIII 51.8 at 6×, both 60 at 1×. The old sleeping-room presentation itself is expensive under extreme software throttling; that remains a limit of this evidence.

These are frozen matched rendering scenes, not a claimed real phone benchmark. Ordinary RAF routes separately validate input, physics and consequences. Dream cloud, decals and decoration caches are bounded; no continuous crystal/refraction work was added.

## Tried, revised, rejected

- Rejected the first full-screen transparent decoration cache after constrained self-QA isolated its cost. Cropped sprites preserve the same authored details and their pixels at a fraction of the blend area.
- Kept the new content inside existing feeding, sleeping, dressing, toy and care verbs. Rejected new rooms, chores, dream gameplay, need meters and a broad acting framework.
- Rejected raw sprite-sheet quadrant cropping: poses crossed the grid midline. Connected-component extraction preserves whole approved candidate silhouettes and soft alpha edges instead.
- Revised kitchen purchase handoff after testing: immediately return Human Fingers to the actual served portion; permanent acquire/equip semantics remain separate.
- Corrected free pantry refill classification so paid treats cannot masquerade as biscuits or suppress their replenishment.
- Used actual dirt clearance for clean contentment; a historical washed flag would have missed repeat baths and could have presented a still-dirty Byte as clean.
- Kept the expressive physical taste grammar. A proposed feeding “failure” was isolated to a test advancing simulation before Chrome had delivered the pointer move. The harness now acknowledges actual pointer dispatch; production mouth/physics authority was not rewritten.
- Ordinary furniture QA initially tapped a chest point occupied by Byte; the corrected route physically moves him into open floor before opening it. A moving-toy catch sampled a stale coordinate, missing the toy and entering the unchanged ladder territory. The route now waits for that thrown body to settle before sampling its next grab. Initial parallel toy throws also exceeded the existing fresh-motion window; isolated input trials preserve real throw momentum. No furniture priority or transition semantics were changed.
- Preserved accessory jank and existing room topology instead of making the campaign a visual reboot.

Remaining attractive ideas—new regions, new toy pillars, bigger preference/memory systems, dream play—cross this mission's boundary. Engine cutoff after this candidate.
