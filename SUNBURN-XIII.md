# Sunburn XIII — a warm miniature world

Experimental candidate based on XII `0352eecb8ee3bd481afb455d276ec18e832c3c6d`. Byte's existing HQ art and physical controllers remain authoritative.

The presentation uses offline rounded Blender geometry with area lighting and authored wood, linen, enamel, brass, glazed ceramic, soil, waxy leaves and ice materials. The browser runs no 3D engine. Static rooms/terrain are cached; moving possessions retain their actual coordinates, rotations, deformable body response and existing hit territories. The bathroom retains its interrogable transparent cutaway, actual water surface, stopper and chain. Crystal optics remain cached and world-relative.

All six rooms, compact passages, ladder/hatches, launch roof, open sky/crystal, backyard house/fence/lawn/paths/trees, mushroom, garden stages, movable furnishings, care tools, foods, toys and trophy presentation receive the shared art family. Wearable art and functional application interfaces retain their established identity.

Validation and exact performance comparison are in progress. This checkpoint is not Human Eyes or handset acceptance.
